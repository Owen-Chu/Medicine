# Anatomical position
* Used as reference for positions on body - reduce ambiguity when identifying parts of the body, precision and accuracy
* Head forward, palms forward, feet pointing forward
![](../Diagrams/Pasted%20image%2020260924155123.png)

## Anterior/posterior
* Anterior (ventral) - front
* Posterior (dorsal) - back
![](../Diagrams/Pasted%20image%2020260924155040.png)

## Superior/inferior and medial/lateral
* Superior (cranial) - top (closer to head)
* Inferior (caudal) - bottom (closer to feet)
* Medial - close to centre
* Lateral - close to ends
![](../Diagrams/Pasted%20image%2020260924155309.png)

## Proximal/distal
* Proximal - closer to torso
* Distal - further from torso
![](../Diagrams/Pasted%20image%2020260924155815.png)

## Superficial/deep
* Superficial - on top
* Deep - beneath
![373](../Diagrams/Pasted%20image%2020260924160028.png)
* **Yellow circle** is said to be <u>superficial</u> to both **red triangle** and **blue square**
* **Red triangle** is said to be <u>deep</u> to **yellow circle** and <u>superficial</u> to **blue square**
* **Blue square** is said to be <u>deep</u> to both **red triangle** and **yellow circle**
