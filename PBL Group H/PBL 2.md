# Clinical Outcomes:

## Discuss the indications for prescribing [[Metformin]] and [[Amlodipine]]

Metformin
1. Indicates [[Type 2 diabetes mellitus |Type 2 diabetes]]

Amlodipine
1. Indicates [[Angina]], [[Hypertension]]

## Discuss the efficacy, safety, suitability and adverse effects of Metformin and Amlodipine

Metformin
1. Reduces gluconeogenesis and increases glucose intake in islet cells
2. Risk for lactic acidosis
3. B<sub>12</sub> deficiency 
4. For use in Talib's case: Talib's Brother will need initially 500mg daily for 1 week, then twice daily for a week, then thrice daily for a week.

Amlodipine
1. Calcium-channel blocker, influences [[Heart |myocardial cells]] which could lead to myocardial contractility to be reduced.
2. [[Asthenia|Lack of physical strength]], Constipation, diarrhoea
3. In Talib's case: Lakia (Talib's Mother) will need initially 5mg once daily. May not be appropriate if there is persistent postural hypotension but as Lakia has hypertension this should not have an effect on the usage of the drug.
