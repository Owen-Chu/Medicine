## Indications:
1. [ Type 2 diabetes](Type%202%20diabetes%20mellitus%20)

## Safety information:
1. No safety concerns in pregnancy
2. Reduced vitamin B<sub>12</sub> levels
3. Risk factor for [Lactic acidosis](Lactic%20acidosis)

## Side effects:
1. [Abdominal pain](Abdominal%20pain)
2. [Appetite decreased](Appetite%20decreased)
3. [Diarrhoea](Diarrhoea)
4. [Nausea](Nausea)
